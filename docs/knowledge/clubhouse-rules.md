# Sunrise Meadows CHS — Clubhouse Rules

## Facilities Covered

The clubhouse hall (Wing C, ground floor), the adjoining gymnasium, and the
terrace garden are shared amenities available to all registered members and
their families.

## Booking the Clubhouse Hall

The hall may be booked for private functions (birthdays, small
celebrations) by members in good standing (no maintenance dues overdue by
more than one month). Bookings are made at least 3 days in advance via the
Society office, or a request can be logged through the AI Secretary
Assistant, which forwards it to the office for confirmation — the AI cannot
confirm a booking itself, only record the request.

- **Booking hours:** 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM.
- **Refundable deposit:** required at time of booking, refunded after
  inspection, minus any damage or excess-cleaning charges.
- **Capacity:** as posted at the hall entrance; exceeding capacity is a
  fire-safety violation.

## Gymnasium

Open 5:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily. Members aged 16 and above may use the
gymnasium; those aged 12–15 may use it only with a parent/guardian present.
Equipment faults should be reported immediately (via WhatsApp to the AI
Secretary Assistant, which logs it as a maintenance ticket) and not
attempted to be self-repaired.

## Terrace Garden

Open 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM. No smoking, no open flames, and no loud music after
9:00 PM. Children below 10 must be accompanied by an adult.

## General Conduct

- Outside food/catering vendors for hall bookings must be pre-approved by
  the office for hygiene and liability reasons.
- Alcohol is not permitted in the clubhouse hall, gymnasium, or terrace
  garden.
- Any damage to clubhouse property is billed to the booking member; disputed
  charges are escalated to the Committee, not resolved by the AI Secretary
  Assistant.
- Persistent misuse of amenities (property damage, repeated rule violations)
  may result in suspension of booking privileges, decided by the Committee.

## Cancellations

Cancellations made at least 48 hours before the booked slot receive a full
deposit refund; cancellations within 48 hours forfeit 50% of the deposit,
per the schedule approved by the Committee.
