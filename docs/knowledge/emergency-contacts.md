# Sunrise Meadows CHS — Emergency Contacts

## On-Site

- **Security Gate (24x7):** +91 98200 55001
- **Facility Manager:** +91 98200 55002
- **Fire Warden (Wing-wise duty roster posted at each lobby notice board)**
- **Society Office (during office hours):** +91 98200 55003

## Utilities

- **Electricity board fault/outage helpline:** 1912 (or local DISCOM number
  posted at the security gate)
- **Water supply / municipal corporation helpline:** posted at the security
  gate and updated by the Facility Manager
- **Cooking gas leak / LPG emergency:** contact the gas supplier's helpline
  printed on the cylinder, and immediately alert the Security Gate

## Emergency Services

- **Police:** 100
- **Fire Brigade:** 101
- **Ambulance:** 108
- **National Emergency Number:** 112
- **Women's Helpline:** 1091
- **Senior Citizen Helpline:** 14567

## Building Emergencies

- **Lift breakdown / entrapment:** call Security Gate immediately, then the
  lift AMC vendor number posted inside each lift car.
- **Fire alarm activation:** evacuate via the nearest staircase (never the
  lift), assemble at the ground-floor muster point near the garden, and
  alert the Fire Warden on duty.
- **Structural or major plumbing emergency (flooding, ceiling collapse
  risk):** contact the Facility Manager and Security Gate immediately; this
  is also logged as an urgent complaint and escalated to the Secretary.

## Reporting an Emergency to the AI Secretary Assistant

For life-threatening emergencies, always call the emergency services above
first. The AI Secretary Assistant can log the incident and notify the
Secretary afterward, but it is not a substitute for calling 100/101/108/112
directly — it must never be relied upon as the first point of contact in an
emergency, and any message describing an active emergency is escalated to
the human Secretary immediately rather than answered as a routine query.
