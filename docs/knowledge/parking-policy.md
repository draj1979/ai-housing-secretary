# Sunrise Meadows CHS — Parking Policy

## Allocation

Each flat is entitled to one covered four-wheeler parking slot and up to two
two-wheeler slots in the designated stack area, allotted by the Committee
based on flat registration order. Allocation is recorded against the
resident's registered vehicle numbers — residents must keep this list
current with the Society office (or via the AI Secretary Assistant) so
security can verify vehicles at the gate.

## Visitor Parking

A limited number of visitor slots near the main gate are available on a
first-come basis for guests staying up to 24 hours. Visitors staying longer,
or arriving frequently, should be registered with the security desk to avoid
towing or clamping.

## Second Vehicle / Additional Slots

A second four-wheeler may be parked in the open compound area, subject to
availability, on payment of the additional parking charge fixed by the
Committee. Additional slots are allotted on a waiting-list basis and are not
guaranteed — the AI Secretary Assistant can record a request but cannot
allocate or confirm a slot; that requires Committee sign-off.

## Prohibited Conduct

- No parking in fire lanes, in front of the DG room, or blocking another
  resident's allotted slot.
- No overnight parking of commercial vehicles, trucks, or trailers without
  prior Committee permission.
- Vehicles must not be washed or repaired in the parking area beyond normal
  cleaning.
- Vehicles parked in violation may be clamped or reported to the Secretary;
  repeat violations are escalated to the Committee.

## Vehicle Registration Changes

Sold, replaced, or additional vehicles must be updated with the Society
within 7 days. Stickers/RFID tags for gate access are reissued only after
the resident record is updated — send the new registration number and a
copy of the RC to the office or via WhatsApp to the AI Secretary Assistant,
which will log the request for the office to action.

## Disputes

Parking slot disputes between residents (e.g., allotment disagreements,
recurring encroachment) are not resolved by the AI Secretary Assistant —
these are logged as complaints and escalated to the Secretary/Committee for
a decision, consistent with the Society's guardrail that only humans make
allocation and enforcement decisions.
