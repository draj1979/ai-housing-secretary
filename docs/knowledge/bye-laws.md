# Sunrise Meadows CHS — Bye-Laws (Summary)

## 1. Membership

A person becomes a member of the Society upon registration of ownership
with the Registrar of Co-operative Societies and payment of the prescribed
membership fee. Membership is transferable only through the Society's
No-Objection Certificate (NOC) process, which requires clearance of all
dues.

## 2. Transfer of Flats

Any sale, gift, or transfer of a flat requires prior intimation to the
Society at least 15 days before the transaction, submission of the transfer
form, and payment of the transfer premium as fixed by the Committee within
statutory limits. The Society cannot unreasonably withhold an NOC once dues
are cleared and documentation is complete.

## 3. Subletting

Members letting out their flat must submit a leave-and-license or rent
agreement copy to the Society office within 15 days of execution, along with
tenant verification documents (police verification is the owner's
responsibility). Unregistered occupants are not permitted.

## 4. Alterations and Structural Changes

No member may carry out structural alterations that affect the building's
safety, common walls, or load-bearing elements without written Committee
approval and, where required, municipal sanction. Non-structural interior
changes do not require Committee approval but must not cause nuisance to
neighbours (e.g., drilling only during permitted hours).

## 5. Common Areas

Corridors, staircases, terraces, and the compound are common property and
must be kept free of personal belongings, footwear racks beyond the
permitted size, or storage. Items left in common areas beyond 48 hours after
a notice may be removed by the Society.

## 6. Pets

Members may keep common household pets (dogs, cats, birds) registered with
the Society office, subject to leash and hygiene rules in common areas.
Aggressive or unregistered animals may be restricted from common areas
pending Committee review.

## 7. Nuisance and Conduct

Members and occupants must not cause noise, nuisance, or disturbance to
other residents, including loud music after 10:00 PM, and must comply with
society rules on functions held in common areas. Repeated or serious
violations — harassment, threats, or conduct requiring police involvement —
are escalated directly to the Secretary and, where applicable, local
authorities; this is not something the AI Secretary Assistant handles or
decides on its own.

## 8. Defaults and Penalties

Non-payment of maintenance beyond 3 months may result in restriction of
voting rights at general meetings and, per statute, may lead to further
recovery action initiated by the Committee. Any change to penalty structure
requires a resolution passed at a general body meeting — not an
administrative or AI decision.

## 9. Amendment of Bye-Laws

These bye-laws may only be amended by a special resolution passed at a
general body meeting and registered with the Registrar of Co-operative
Societies, per applicable state co-operative society rules.
