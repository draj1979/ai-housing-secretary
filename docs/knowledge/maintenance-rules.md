# Sunrise Meadows CHS — Maintenance Rules

## Reporting a Maintenance Issue

Residents can report maintenance issues (plumbing, electrical, lift,
common-area repairs, pest control, etc.) via WhatsApp to the AI Secretary
Assistant at any time. Each report is logged with a ticket ID, the flat
number, and a description, and routed to the Facility Manager. Residents
receive their ticket ID immediately and can check status by referencing it.

## Response Timelines (Indicative)

- **Common-area emergencies** (lift entrapment, major leakage, power
  failure in common areas): attended within 1–2 hours.
- **Urgent in-flat issues** (no water supply, electrical fault): attended
  within 24 hours.
- **Routine requests** (minor plumbing, carpentry, painting touch-ups):
  attended within 3–5 working days, subject to vendor availability.

These are indicative service targets, not guarantees, and depend on vendor
availability and the scope of work. The AI Secretary Assistant cannot
commit to a specific completion time or approve extra spend beyond routine
repairs — those decisions rest with the Facility Manager/Committee.

## Scope of Society-Funded Maintenance

The Society's maintenance fund covers common-area upkeep, structural
repairs, common plumbing and electrical lines, lifts, and shared amenities.
It does not cover in-flat fixtures, appliances, or interior fittings, which
remain the owner's responsibility, except where a common-area fault (e.g., a
leaking common riser) causes damage inside a flat.

## Sinking Fund and Major Repairs

A portion of monthly maintenance is set aside in the sinking fund for major
repairs (repainting, waterproofing, structural repairs) as decided by the
Committee and approved at the general body meeting. Residents cannot
individually direct how sinking fund money is spent — that is a Committee
and general-body decision, not something the AI Secretary Assistant can
approve or change.

## Housekeeping and Common-Area Cleaning

Common areas are cleaned daily; garbage is collected wing-wise each morning
per the schedule posted at each lobby. Residents should segregate wet and
dry waste as required by municipal rules — non-compliance may be logged as
a complaint against the flat if it repeatedly affects other residents.

## Vendor and Contractor Access

Residents' personal contractors (for in-flat work) must register with
security and are not automatically covered by the Society's maintenance
staff. Any request to bring in outside vendors for common-area work must go
through the Facility Manager, not be arranged informally by a resident.
