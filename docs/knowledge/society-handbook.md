# Sunrise Meadows CHS — Society Handbook

## About the Society

Sunrise Meadows Co-operative Housing Society ("the Society") is a registered
housing co-operative comprising 5 wings (A through E) and 240 flats. The
Society is managed by a Managing Committee elected annually at the Annual
General Meeting (AGM), and day-to-day operations are coordinated by the
Society Secretary.

## Office Hours

The Society office is open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM and
4:00 PM to 7:00 PM. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays declared by
the Committee. Residents may also reach the AI Secretary Assistant on
WhatsApp 24x7 for routine queries, complaints, and suggestions; matters
requiring a decision are forwarded to the human Secretary.

## Membership and Occupancy

Every flat owner is a member of the Society and must register their contact
details, vehicle numbers, and an emergency contact with the office within
30 days of possession or transfer. Tenants must be registered via the
standard tenancy intimation form, co-signed by the owner, before moving in.

## Committee Structure

The Managing Committee consists of a Chairperson, Secretary, Treasurer, and
up to 5 additional members, each serving a two-year term. Committee meetings
are held on the first Sunday of every month; minutes are circulated to all
members within 7 days.

## Maintenance Charges

Monthly maintenance is billed on the 1st of every month and due by the 10th.
Charges are calculated per the area-based formula approved at the AGM and
cover housekeeping, security, common-area electricity, the sinking fund, and
repair reserve. Late payment beyond the 10th attracts interest as fixed by
the Committee from time to time — the AI Secretary Assistant cannot waive,
discount, or change this amount; any dispute is escalated to the Treasurer.

## Common Amenities

The Society maintains a garden, children's play area, a multipurpose
clubhouse hall, and a gymnasium on the ground floor of Wing C. Amenity
bookings and rules are covered in the separate Clubhouse Rules document.

## Communication Channels

Official announcements are sent via the WhatsApp broadcast group and posted
on the notice board near the main gate. Residents should not rely on
informal wing-level groups for official notices — only messages from the
Secretary's registered number or the AI Secretary Assistant, once approved
by the Secretary, are considered official.

## Grievance Redressal

Residents may raise complaints or suggestions at any time via WhatsApp. Each
complaint receives a ticket ID and is tracked until resolution. Complaints
that cannot be resolved within a reasonable time, or that involve legal,
financial, or committee-level decisions, are escalated to the Secretary and
Committee directly — the AI Secretary Assistant only records and routes
these; it does not resolve or approve them itself.
